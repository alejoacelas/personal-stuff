- Alejo asked for high-wattage lights like those on [meaningness.com/sad-light-lumens](https://meaningness.com/sad-light-lumens#MOAR), ranked by watts per pound.
- Search results: the best real watts per pound in the UK are Ledvion floodlights from Lightexpert (Dutch retailer with a UK site, duty-paid, free delivery from £50). The 150W 6500K LV50066 costs £19.99 (13p/W, rated 110 lm/W, CRI >80, 3-year warranty, checked in stock 8 Oct). The Osram-chip 100W LV50010 costs £14.99 and has 258 reviews at 4.7. Toolstation's V-TAC 100W and 200W floodlights are next at 20–23p/W, on sale. Floodlights come with a bare cable and need a 13 A plug fitted (3 A fuse covers up to about 700 W).
- Amazon UK's cheapest per claimed watt were "deformable" E27 garage lights (about £18 for a claimed 200 W), but no one has measured them and similar lights draw a fraction of their claim. Cheap Amazon UK floods and UFO high bays cost 14–18p per claimed watt from unknown brands.
- Search-method lesson: the only measured lumenator data found (a DragonLight 100W corn bulb drawing 65 W; eBay floodlights drawing under half) says rated watts are inflated, so buy one light plus a plug-in power meter and check before buying the rest. UK lamp bases are rated about 60 W, so corn bulbs are a poor fit here.

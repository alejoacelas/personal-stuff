## Keep a log of what Alejo says

Append to [log.md](log.md) whenever Alejo gives a fact, preference or decision about buying or owning things, or when something is bought. Date each entry and quote or closely paraphrase him. The log is context for future sessions and is not yet endorsed.

When Alejo reviews an entry and endorses it, move its content into the official documents at the repository root (`inputs.md`, `recommendations.md`, `DECISIONS.md` or `inventory.md`) and delete it from the log.

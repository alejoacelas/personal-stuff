# Questions

These are answers that would change a recommendation substantially. Each question names the group it affects in the Recommendations tab.

1. **Where can the disassembled closet and bed-frame panels go?** If they won't fit flat against a wall, disassembling them frees little space. (1. Free up the room)
2. **What kind of mattress is it: spring or foam, and how thick?** A floppy mattress won't stand against a wall without support, and a heavy one is a chore to lay down each time you lounge. (1, 2)
3. **What cooktop does the kitchen have?** On induction, the wok must be flat-bottomed and the pans induction-ready. (5. Kitchen)
4. **Is the kitchen shared, and what cookware and knives are already there?** You might not need the pot, the pan or a second knife. (5)
5. **How many guests, and where do they sit?** More than three guests means more plates, bowls and seating. (5)
6. **Does your door frame have moulding, and does the tenancy allow marks on it?** This decides between the leverage bar and the tension bar. (6. Exercise)
7. **Which bike do you have, and does it have mounting points for a rear rack?** Without them, the rack is out and the basket or backpack wins. (7. Carrying)
8. **How long will you stay in this flat, and when are you next in Colombia?** A short stay favours things that pack small. A trip soon means you can bring back the sunrise lamp and anything else useful. (all groups)
